# FsGAN
This project focuses on few-shot image generation and AI-assisted cervical cancer diagnosis based on MRI images. The dataset is derived from real clinical imaging data collected in hospitals, which provides strong practical relevance and clinical value for the research.

The proposed framework aims to address the challenges of limited medical imaging data by leveraging few-shot image generation techniques to augment training samples and improve the performance of deep learning models. Through the integration of generated images and transfer learning strategies, the project seeks to enhance the accuracy and robustness of cervical cancer diagnosis.

By making the code and research results publicly available, this project hopes to support researchers, students, and developers working in the fields of medical imaging, computer vision, and healthcare AI. Ultimately, the research may contribute to the development of more reliable AI-assisted diagnostic systems, helping clinicians make more informed decisions and improving access to advanced medical technologies.

Dataset
Real-world cervical cancer MRI data collected from clinical hospitals.
Expert annotations provided by experienced radiologists.
Multi-parameter pharmacokinetic MRI images generated from DCE-MRI sequences.
Designed for research on few-shot learning, image generation, and medical image classification.
Potential Impact
Improve diagnostic performance under limited-data conditions.
Reduce the dependency of deep learning models on large-scale medical datasets.
Facilitate research in AI-assisted healthcare and medical image analysis.
Promote the development of accessible and trustworthy diagnostic support systems.
